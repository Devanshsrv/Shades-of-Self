# Shades of Self

## About the Game
Shades of Self is a 2D narrative-driven platformer built around emotional healing and self-discovery. The game transforms emotions into gameplay mechanics, environments, and player interactions rather than presenting them only through dialogue or cutscenes.

The player journeys through symbolic worlds representing different emotional states such as Denial, Loneliness, Guilt, Anger, and Hope. Each level is designed so that its mechanics, visuals, lighting, and atmosphere reflect the psychology of that emotion.

Examples include:
- Reality shifting between illusion and truth in Denial
- Navigating darkness using a fragile guiding Wisp in Loneliness
- A dynamic light system in Guilt that reacts to player actions
- Rage-based combat mechanics in Anger

The game focuses on understanding emotions rather than defeating them.

---

## Aim of the Project
The aim of Shades of Self is to explore how emotional experiences can be translated into interactive gameplay systems and symbolic level design.

The project focuses on:
- Emotional storytelling through mechanics
- Symbolic and atmosphere-driven environments
- Immersive player experiences
- Gameplay tied to psychological themes
- Narrative expression through interaction

- 2D platforming and movement systems
